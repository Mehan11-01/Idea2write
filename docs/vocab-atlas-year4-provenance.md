# Year 4 photographic vocabulary atlases

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, one call for each of the 12 atlas files. Each atlas is a single 3-column ×4-row contact sheet of 12 separate photographic scenes in the original vocabulary order. The six legacy missions use their first 10 cells; the remaining cells are distinct spare scenes. All boards were visually checked for order, photographic style, grid geometry and duplicates. Final image dimensions:1086×1448; each cell362×362.

Prompt specification used for every atlas: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a SINGLE vocabulary PHOTO ATLAS image for an educational website. Produce ONE portrait contact sheet, EXACTLY THREE COLUMNS by FOUR ROWS, 12 equal square panels, total aspect ratio3:4. Panels tile edge-to-edge at exact thirds horizontally and exact quarters vertically, no gutters/margins/borders/labels/captions/text overlays. Twelve completely different genuine camera photographs, not a single scene extending across cells. Every panel has its own distinct composition, background and subject/action. Natural real textures and lighting, Malaysian upper-primary educational context, age-appropriate modest full clothing, no illustration/cartoon/CGI/watermarks. Main action or subject central within each cell. Strict row-major left-to-right order.” The per-panel scene briefs, preserving the prompt’s meaning and order, are recorded in vocab-atlas-year4-scenes.json.
