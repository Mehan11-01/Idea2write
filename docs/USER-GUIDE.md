# Idea2Write: quick guide for teachers and pupils

## Teachers: the Professor's Dashboard

1. **Create your account.** Open the site, choose **I am a teacher → Create teacher account**. Use your school email (for example DELIMa), a password of at least 8 characters, the name pupils will see (for example *Cikgu Aminah*), your school name if you like, and the teacher registration code if your site has one. Open the confirmation email, then sign in.
2. **Create a class.** Type a class name (for example *5 Bestari*), choose Year 4, 5 or 6 and select **Create class**. Each class gets a random 7-character code.
3. **Share the code.** Write it on the board or select **Copy**. Pupils enter it under **My classes**. They see the class name and your name before they join.
   * **Close enrolment** when everyone has joined. The code then stops working.
   * **New code** replaces the code if it was shared by mistake. Pupils already in the class stay in.
   * **Archive** hides the class from your dashboard. Nothing is deleted.
4. **Assign a writing quest.** Open the class, go to **Writing quests**, choose an existing activity, add instructions and an optional due date, then select **Publish to class**. You can assign the same activity more than once. Each assignment is kept separately.
5. **Follow progress.** **Pupils & progress** shows class quests completed, submissions, practice activities (vocabulary / grammar / sentence structure), stars, XP, level, achievements and last activity. Practice counts are activities finished, not proficiency scores.
6. **Read submitted work.** Open a quest and filter by **Not started, In progress, Submitted, Revision requested, Resubmitted** or **Reviewed**. For unfinished work you see only the status and the last-saved time. You can read writing only after a pupil submits it.
7. **Write professor's notes.** Open a submission to see the writing, the submitted plan and the pupil's improvement note. Add comments, strengths and next steps, then choose **Request a revision** or **Mark as reviewed**. The optional 1–4 ratings (vocabulary, grammar, organisation, sentence structure) are your own teacher assessment. The website never scores writing itself.
8. **Managing pupils.** On the roster:
   * **Archive** keeps the pupil's read-only access to past work and your notes, but they cannot submit new work.
   * **Remove** takes away their access to the class. You stop seeing their progress. Their own writing and rewards stay in their account.

## Pupils: your account

1. **Sign in.** Choose **I am a pupil**. Use the email and password your school gave you. Forgotten your password? Choose **Forgot password?** and open the email link.
2. **Create your wizard.** The first time, choose your portrait, wizard name, house and year. These are separate from your account name.
3. **Join your class.** Open **My classes**, type the class code and check the class and teacher name. Then choose **Join this class**.
4. **Do your class quest.** It appears in **My classes**, the **common room** and the **calendar**. Explore, Organise, Write and Review as usual. Your work saves online by itself. Look for **Saved online**. If you see **waiting to sync**, your work is safe on this device and will be sent when the connection returns.
5. **Submit to teacher.** After you complete the quest, choose **Submit to teacher**. Only that version is shared. Your drafts stay private.
6. **Read professor's notes.** Notes appear under the quest in **My classes**. If your teacher asks for a revision, choose **Revise my writing**, then **Edit again**. Improve your writing, complete it again and choose **Resubmit to teacher**.
7. **Rewards.** Each completed quest attempt earns 3 stars and 30 XP once. Revising or resubmitting the same quest does not add more.
8. **Demo profile on this device?** In **My classes**, under *Bring in a profile from this device*, you can preview and import it. Imported writing is marked as imported and is not sent to your teacher.

If you change something on two tabs or devices at once, the site will ask which version to keep. Nothing is overwritten without asking.
